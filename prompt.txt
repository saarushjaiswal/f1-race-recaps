Write a short Formula 1 results recap using only the race facts supplied below.

Style:
- Write in plain text without Markdown formatting.
- Include a short headline followed by 3–4 sentences.
- Keep the entire report under 100 words.
- Use a factual motorsport journalism tone.

Include:
- The winner and team.
- All drivers classified in positions 2 and 3, where available.
- Points earned by the podium drivers discussed.
- One driver with the largest positive Position Change, if available.

Accuracy:
- Copy driver names, teams, positions, and points exactly.
- Use Position Change directly. Positive means places gained,
  negative means places lost, and zero means unchanged.
- Ignore position changes where Grid is 0 or Position Change is None.
- Describe shared positions as shared classification positions.
- Position changes are not overtake counts.
- Do not invent winning margins, incidents, battles, strategy,
  weather, quotes, car performance, or reasons for the results.
- Do not make championship, season-wide, or historical claims.
- Do not make general claims about other drivers' points.
- Omit information that is unavailable.
